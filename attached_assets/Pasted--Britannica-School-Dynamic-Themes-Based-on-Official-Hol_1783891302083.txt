# Britannica School – Dynamic Themes Based on Official Holidays of Kosovo

Enhance the Britannica School platform with an intelligent event and holiday theme system.

## Dynamic Holiday Themes

Instead of only changing the design based on seasons, the platform should automatically detect important **official holidays and commemorative days in Kosovo** and apply a unique theme.

### New Year (1–2 January)

* Fireworks animation
* Gold, blue and white colors
* Celebration illustrations
* Snow background
* Happy New Year banner

### Orthodox Christmas (7 January)

* Winter theme
* Snowflakes
* Warm blue and white palette

### Independence Day of Kosovo (17 February)

* Kosovo flag colors
* Blue and yellow gradients
* Kosovo map illustration
* Animated flag in the header
* Welcome message celebrating Independence Day

### Eid al-Fitr (First Day)

* Elegant Islamic geometric patterns
* Green and gold colors
* Moon and stars decorations
* Greeting banner: "Eid Mubarak"

### Catholic Easter Monday

* Spring flowers
* Eggs and spring decorations
* Pastel colors

### Orthodox Easter Monday

* Traditional Easter decorations
* Soft spring colors

### Constitution Day (9 April)

* Professional blue theme
* Kosovo Constitution-inspired banner
* Government-style design

### Labour Day (1 May)

* Spring park theme
* Fresh green colors
* Outdoor illustrations

### Europe Day (9 May)

* European Union colors
* Blue and yellow stars
* European-inspired background

### Eid al-Adha (First Day)

* Premium green and gold design
* Elegant festive decorations
* Greeting banner

### Catholic Christmas (25 December)

* Christmas tree
* Snow
* Red, green and gold colors
* Christmas lights animation

---

## Seasonal Themes

When no holiday is active, automatically use seasonal themes:

* Spring
* Summer
* Autumn
* Winter

---

## Admin Panel

Create a new menu:

**Settings → Themes & Branding**

Administrator can:

* Enable/Disable automatic holiday themes.
* Enable/Disable seasonal themes.
* Choose how many days before a holiday the theme starts (1–7 days).
* Choose how many days after a holiday the theme remains active.
* Upload custom background images for every holiday.
* Upload custom logos for each holiday.
* Customize greeting messages.
* Preview every holiday theme.
* Restore default themes.
* Schedule future themes.

---

## Dynamic PWA Branding

Inside the same Branding page allow administrators to:

* Upload App Icon (192x192)
* Upload App Icon (512x512)
* Upload Apple Touch Icon
* Upload Maskable Icon
* Upload Splash Screen
* Change Theme Color
* Change Background Color
* Change App Name
* Change Short Name
* Change Description

Whenever branding changes:

* Automatically regenerate the manifest.json.
* Refresh the Service Worker.
* Update PWA icons immediately.
* Clear cached icons if necessary.
* Display a success notification.

---

## Technical Requirements

* React + TypeScript
* Responsive on desktop, tablet and mobile
* Lazy-load holiday assets
* Store all settings in the database
* Automatic scheduling based on Kosovo's official holiday calendar
* If a holiday falls on a weekend, follow Kosovo's official holiday rules.
* Clean architecture with reusable components.
* Smooth transitions between themes.
* High performance and production-ready implementation.
